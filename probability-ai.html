Uncertainty is omnipresent—from stock movements and medical prognoses to autonomous driving path planning. AI-powered systems do not eliminate uncertainty; they master it through probabilistic reasoning. This unit explores how intelligent agents quantify uncertainty, calculate sample spaces, evaluate mutually exclusive and overlapping events via set operations, and fuse sensor telemetry (optical cameras, LiDAR, radar) to make safe, optimal real-time decisions.

Environmental Risk Parameters

Traffic Congestion Risk $P(\text{Traffic})$: 18%

Airport Idle Wait Disutility Weight: 1.5x
Meteorite Collision Risk: $P(\text{Meteorite}) = 0.0000001\%$. The AI model notes this outcome exists in the sample space, but assigns it negligible decision weight.
Plan A: Leave 90 Min Ahead AI Recommendation
  • • Expected Wait at Terminal: ~22 mins
  • • Risk of Flight Delay/Miss: 1.8%
  • • Expected Total Loss Score: 34.2

Plan B: Leave 180 Min Ahead Over-Conservative
  • • Expected Wait at Terminal: ~112 mins
  • • Risk of Flight Delay/Miss: 0.05%
  • • Expected Total Loss Score: 168.0

Virtually eliminates flight miss risk, but causes a nearly 2-hour unproductive wait penalty.
